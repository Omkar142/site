# Omkar — engineering, delivery and AI

A recruiter-facing character portfolio: oversized typography, a consistent detailed character, early interactive codepad, résumé-grounded impact and four visible expandable work stories. The reading journey is hero → codepad → impact → selected work, followed by the motion/technical-range chapters, delivery practice, experience and contact.

Google Play, Tracxn and Cisco titles, dates, metrics and attribution follow the résumé supplied on October 11. ScreenGod is the third story, with a public Google Play link and actual product screenshot; Cisco remains the fourth engineering story. Gemini/RAG/Crashlytics/telemetry details come from the supplied résumé. No independent performance audit of career results or ScreenGod internals is claimed.

The header, hero and contact expose the supplied résumé URL. Contact provides Gmail compose with a prefilled recipient, a prominent LinkedIn profile link, copy-email fallback and a conventional mailto link. These are links, not message-sending integrations.

The hero uses transparent footage with natural idle and real hand gestures. A short scroll chapter keeps the gesture in view while presenting a right-side Google, Tracxn, Cisco and ScreenGod panel. The source artwork is mirrored so the open hand presents toward the panel. Company cards crossfade and rise in direct proportion to scroll distance, without timed transitions or waiting for frame downloads; accessible selectors also drive the pose and case-study link. Desktop uses 320px of extra travel; compact layouts pin only the character for 240px. Short screens, reduced motion, motion-off and data saver retain a reading layout. Pointer transforms are bounded; replay is keyboard accessible. These are prerecorded gestures, not independently rigged limb tracking. A matching production-quality live 3D character needs a separate modeled and articulated asset; this revision does not claim to convert the film into a 3D mesh.

Mindset and contact use the same detailed character, lazy idle playback, a once-per-section filmed greeting and responses to their section links. Gesture bytes are shared and paused offscreen/hidden. The codepad adds one character every 34ms, with punctuation and line pauses. Layered orange, steel and cobalt paint strokes lead for 1100ms into the approved typographic splash, which holds 900ms and opens with a 550ms curtain reveal (2900ms failsafe), once per session per splash revision; deep links, reduced motion, motion-off and data saver skip it. It never blocks navigation or waits for asset downloads.

ScreenGod leads with an explicitly illustrative screen-context → model/retrieval → answer diagram. The original published screenshot is available in a secondary disclosure. The diagram does not claim to depict the shipped app's exact interface.

Buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript with a bundled Three.js scene; self-hosted fonts and visual assets. Official Google/Tracxn wordmark sources and integrity are recorded in assets/brand-sources.json. The responsive reading policy handles ordinary phones and tall touch viewports with desktop-like width. No credentials or analytics are included. The three employer illustrations are conceptual diagrams; ScreenGod has an illustrative product-flow panel and an original published screenshot in its evidence disclosure.

Run `npm test`, `npm run build:scene` and `python tests/static-check.py`. See QA.md: logic/geometry/static checks do not verify browser layout, GPU appearance, device frame rate or whether a visitor is signed into Gmail/Drive.

The GitHub Pages ZIP places the site at the repository root with CNAME for www.omkarg.co.in. GitHub publication requires repository access; the Sites deployment is separate.
