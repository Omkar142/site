### Seated hero — prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium website mascot cutout. Create an original, beautifully art-directed 3D character for Omkar's engineering, technical-program-management and AI portfolio. A charming compact robot systems pilot, large smooth ivory ceramic rounded-rectangle helmet/head with glossy smoked-black glass faceplate, two subtle horizontal pale-cyan eye lights, tiny orange antenna tab. Oversized rich cobalt-blue padded technical utility jacket with cream collar and a small orange square chest patch, cobalt articulated trousers, graphite hands, chunky ivory sneakers with warm burnt-orange sole details. It is a design-object mascot with tactile high-end materials, physically convincing reflections and fabric folds, not a cheap toy, not humanoid photo, not any existing robot franchise. Full-body confident seated pose on a single translucent cobalt-glass rectangular plinth, one hand resting on knee, one elbow relaxed, feet facing slightly left. Three-quarter view looking toward camera-left, restrained friendly confidence. Studio CGI campaign render with large softbox key top left, blue rim light and warm highlights, incredibly polished materials. Transparent background, entire character and plinth included, clean alpha edges. Square 1400x1400 composition, generous 10% transparent margin, subject centered and fills 80% of canvas. No text, no logo, no watermark, no environment, no floor outside plinth. Never a penguin, never an astronaut in a spacesuit. Original creative direction inspired by character-led portfolio websites.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: abstract architectural sculpture hero illustration for a premium technical program manager portfolio. Render a single elegant 3D sculpture made of two interlocking open rectangular limestone arches and one translucent deep forest green glass curved ribbon passing through them, with a small pale green glass bridge joining the two. Precise architectural minimalism, stone microtexture and realistic glass refraction. At a 3/4 isometric angle, whole object visible occupying 80% of frame, light studio lighting from top left, soft grounded shadow. Solid seamless background color exactly #f2f4ed. Square high resolution composition, object in center with generous clearance. No text, logos, people, objects outside sculpture, gradient backdrop, purple, blue, holograms, floating UI. A contemporary architectural art object suggesting connecting people and systems. This is an abstract decorative illustration, not a scientific diagram.
